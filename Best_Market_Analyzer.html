How the ranking works
  • Trend persistence (25%) — % of adjacent closed 1-min candles that closed in the same direction. Higher = trends run instead of chopping.
  • Candle strength (20%) — % of the last 30 closed candles whose body was ≥ 30% of their range — the exact “no-doji” test your bot’s scanner demands.
  • Directional efficiency (20%) — net travel ÷ path travelled over the last 30 candles. High = price actually goes somewhere.
  • Tick-stream momentum (15%) — how consistently the 10-tick momentum direction has held over the last 100 ticks — your bot’s check #4, measured over time.
  • Volatility (20%) — average 1-min range as % of price. 5-tick contracts need movement; rank-based so slow indices never score 0 by units.
  • Scores are weighted percentile ranks within this scan (0–100). The RF badge mirrors your Rise/Fall bot’s 4 scanner checks exactly (side = last closed candle; live candle agrees; tick beyond that close; body ≥ 0.3×range; 10-tick stream). The EO badge mirrors the Even/Odd bot’s parity gate: last digit’s parity = parity 10 ticks back (anchor) and ≥ 6 of the last 10 digits agreeing — READY when both pass.
